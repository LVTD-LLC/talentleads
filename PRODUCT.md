# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

TalentLeads is for startup founders, operators, and recruiting teams who need to find credible technical talent without running a traditional inbound hiring funnel. The near-term audience is human B2B buyers evaluating whether the product is trustworthy and useful. The product direction is AI-agent-first: agents should eventually be able to search and act through MCP, while humans supervise strategy, access, and outcomes.

## Product Purpose

TalentLeads helps companies identify relevant candidates, review useful profile context, and start outreach faster than job boards or manual sourcing. Success means a buyer can quickly understand the database, trust the quality of candidates, and see a path from search to outreach without friction.

## Brand Personality

Sharp, trustworthy, efficient. The interface should feel simple and modern with a restrained futuristic edge, closer to Linear than to a loud startup landing page.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS gradients, decorative AI-purple glow, bloated card grids, vague automation copy, and anything that makes the product feel like a toy. Do not over-index on agent jargon in the visual language; agent-readiness should feel operational and credible.

## Design Principles

- Lead with buyer confidence: show useful proof, clear workflows, and calm interface details before hype.
- Make the product feel agent-ready without becoming abstract: emphasize structured search, clean data, and direct actions.
- Keep the surface quiet and fast: visual polish should improve scanning, not slow down evaluation.
- Use specificity over buzzwords: describe what TalentLeads does in concrete sourcing and outreach terms.
- Preserve trust at every step: forms, navigation, and profile views should feel stable and deliberate.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for WCAG AA contrast, visible keyboard focus, responsive layouts across mobile and desktop, and reduced-motion-safe interactions. Motion should be subtle and should never gate content visibility.
